# The East Annex

Mission 02: `/mischief.html?route=annex&renderer=pixel`.

This is an expanded vault job sharing the original approach, not a separate biome or a procedural level. The world grows from 4300 to 5350 units. Five new platforms provide an ascending crossing and a lower recovery ledge. The Sun moves to x5050; a third seal sits on the highest annex roof at x4700. All three seals are required. Optional music moves to x4450, so taking it means carrying noise through the return rather than collecting it near home.

Two additional steam vents protect the east service walk. They retain the same warning and safe intervals, displayed using the collision configuration. Rooftops pass above their damage band. On return, wait for a clear interval or use upper platforms. The original street and restored-lift escape choices remain available. No new buttons, altered movement, extra wallet requirement, rewards or permanent completion claims.

Home and pause menus link both pixel missions. The map and touch targets use the mission's actual seals, vents and room width. Retry resets the third seal, relocated loot and all mission state.
